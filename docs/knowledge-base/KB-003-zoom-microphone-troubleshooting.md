# KB-003 — Zoom Microphone Troubleshooting

**Category:** Communication  
**System:** Zoom  
**Audience:** Helpdesk  
**Severity:** Medium  
**Last Updated:** 2026-10-06

## Problem

Users may experience microphone problems during Zoom meetings.

The issue may prevent the user from being heard by other
participants or may cause Zoom to use the wrong audio input
device.

## Symptoms

- Other participants cannot hear the user.
- Zoom shows that no microphone is detected.
- The microphone works in another application but not in Zoom.
- The wrong microphone is selected in Zoom.
- The microphone is muted at the operating system level.
- The microphone works intermittently.
- The user may receive an error related to microphone access.

## Troubleshooting Steps

1. Confirm whether the microphone works in another application.
2. Confirm that the correct microphone is physically connected to
   the computer.
3. Check the Windows sound settings and verify that the microphone
   is detected.
4. Verify that the microphone input level is responding to the
   user's voice.
5. Confirm that Windows microphone permissions allow applications
   to access the microphone.
6. Open Zoom audio settings.
7. Verify that the correct microphone is selected.
8. Use Zoom's microphone test to confirm that audio input is being
   detected.
9. Check whether the microphone is muted in Zoom or through the
   physical device controls.
10. Test the microphone in a Zoom meeting.
11. If possible, test with another known-working microphone.
12. If the issue persists, document the results of the tests and
    determine whether the problem is related to the hardware,
    Windows, permissions, or Zoom.

## Common Causes

Common causes of Zoom microphone issues include:

- The wrong microphone is selected in Zoom.
- The microphone is muted in Zoom or on the physical device.
- Windows does not detect the microphone correctly.
- Windows microphone permissions are disabled.
- Another application is using the microphone.
- Outdated or incorrect audio drivers.
- A faulty USB connection or cable.
- A defective microphone or headset.
- Incorrect Zoom audio settings.
- Temporary application or operating system issues.

## Resolution

The resolution depends on the identified cause.

Possible resolutions include:

- Selecting the correct microphone in Zoom.
- Enabling microphone permissions in Windows.
- Unmuting the microphone in Zoom or through the physical device
  controls.
- Reconnecting the microphone or headset.
- Testing a different USB port or connection.
- Updating or reinstalling the appropriate audio driver.
- Correcting Zoom audio configuration.
- Closing another application that is using the microphone.
- Replacing a faulty microphone or headset when hardware failure
  is confirmed.

After applying the resolution, Helpdesk should test the microphone
in Zoom and confirm that other participants can hear the user
clearly.

## When to Escalate

Escalate the ticket when:

- The microphone is not detected by Windows after basic troubleshooting.
- Hardware failure is confirmed and a replacement is required.
- The issue requires administrator access to modify drivers or system settings.
- Multiple users or devices are affected.
- A Zoom service issue is suspected.
- The problem persists after standard troubleshooting.
- Security, privacy, or organizational policy restrictions may be involved.

## Information Required for Escalation

Include the following information when escalating the ticket:

- User name and department.
- Ticket number.
- Device model and operating system.
- Microphone or headset make/model, if known.
- Zoom version, if relevant.
- Whether the microphone works in other applications.
- Whether Windows detects the microphone.
- Windows microphone permission status.
- Microphone selected in Zoom.
- Exact error message, if any.
- Troubleshooting steps performed and their results.
- Screenshots or other relevant evidence.
- Business impact on the user.
