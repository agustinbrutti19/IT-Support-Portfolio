# KB-005 — Laptop Performance Troubleshooting

**Category:** Hardware / Performance  
**System:** Windows  
**Audience:** Helpdesk  
**Severity:** Medium  
**Last Updated:** 2026-10-06

## Problem

Users may experience slow or unresponsive laptop performance while performing normal work activities.

The issue may affect applications, system responsiveness, startup time, or the user's ability to complete daily tasks efficiently.

## Symptoms

Common symptoms may include:

- Applications take longer than expected to open.
- Windows becomes slow or temporarily unresponsive.
- The laptop takes a long time to start.
- Applications freeze or stop responding.
- The user experiences delays when switching between applications.
- High CPU, memory, or disk usage is observed.
- The laptop becomes unusually hot or the fan runs continuously.
- Performance decreases significantly when multiple applications are open.
- The issue occurs only with specific applications or tasks.
- The problem occurs intermittently or at specific times.

## Troubleshooting Steps

1. Confirm when the performance issue started.
2. Determine whether the issue is constant or intermittent.
3. Identify which applications or tasks are affected.
4. Check whether the issue affects only the user's laptop or multiple devices.
5. Restart the laptop and verify whether the issue persists.
6. Check available disk space.
7. Open Task Manager and review CPU, memory, and disk usage.
8. Identify applications or processes consuming unusually high resources.
9. Check which applications are configured to start automatically with Windows.
10. Confirm that Windows and required applications are up to date.
11. Check whether the laptop is unusually hot or the cooling fan is running continuously.
12. Close unnecessary applications and background processes when appropriate.
13. Test the affected application again.
14. Document the findings and determine whether the issue is related to software, system resources, hardware, or another factor.

## Common Causes

Common causes may include:

- Insufficient available RAM.
- High CPU usage caused by an application or background process.
- High disk usage.
- Low available disk space.
- Too many applications running at the same time.
- Excessive startup applications.
- Pending Windows or application updates.
- Outdated or problematic device drivers.
- Malware or other unwanted software.
- Overheating or inadequate cooling.
- Hardware degradation or failure.
- Application-specific performance issues.
- Temporary Windows or application problems.

## Resolution

The resolution depends on the identified cause.

Possible resolutions include:

- Closing unnecessary applications or background processes.
- Disabling unnecessary startup applications when appropriate.
- Freeing disk space.
- Installing pending Windows or application updates.
- Updating or reinstalling problematic device drivers.
- Removing unwanted or malicious software according to company procedures.
- Correcting application-specific configuration issues.
- Resolving overheating or cooling problems.
- Replacing faulty hardware when hardware failure is confirmed.
- Escalating issues that require administrator access or specialized hardware support.

After applying the resolution, Helpdesk should retest the laptop and confirm that system performance has returned to an acceptable level for the user's normal work activities.

## When to Escalate

Escalate the ticket when:

- The issue persists after standard troubleshooting.
- Hardware failure is suspected or confirmed.
- The laptop shows signs of overheating or physical damage.
- The issue requires administrator access beyond Helpdesk permissions.
- Windows or device drivers cannot be updated or repaired through standard procedures.
- Malware or security concerns are identified.
- Multiple users or devices experience similar performance problems.
- The issue may be caused by a broader Windows, application, or infrastructure problem.
- The laptop requires hardware replacement or specialized technical support.

## Information Required for Escalation

Include the following information when escalating the ticket:

- User name and department.
- Ticket number.
- Device make and model.
- Operating system and version.
- Date and time the issue started.
- Description of the performance problem.
- Applications or tasks affected.
- CPU, memory, and disk usage observed during the issue.
- Available disk space.
- Relevant Windows or application error messages.
- Recent software, driver, or hardware changes.
- Troubleshooting steps performed and their results.
- Any signs of overheating or unusual hardware behavior.
- Screenshots or other relevant evidence.
- Business impact on the user or department.
