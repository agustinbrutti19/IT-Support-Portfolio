# Ticket 001 — CRM Access Denied

## Ticket Information

- **Ticket ID:** 001
- **User:** Sarah Johnson
- **Department:** Reservations
- **Issue Type:** Access / Permissions
- **Priority:** High
- **Status:** Resolved
- **Assigned To:** Helpdesk

## User Report

Sarah Johnson reports that she is unable to access the CRM.
When attempting to log in, the application displays an
"Access Denied" message.

## Initial Assessment

The issue appears to be related to user access or permissions.
The user's CRM account and assigned permissions should be
verified before making any changes.

## Troubleshooting Steps

1. Verified that Sarah's user account is active.
2. Confirmed that Sarah's Microsoft 365 account is working correctly.
3. Verified that Sarah's CRM permissions were missing.
4. Confirmed that Sarah was not a member of the required CRM access group.
5. Added Sarah to the appropriate CRM access group.
6. Asked Sarah to sign out and sign in again.
7. Confirmed that Sarah was able to access the CRM successfully.

## Root Cause

The user's account was active, but she was missing the required
CRM access group membership.

## Resolution

Sarah was added to the appropriate CRM access group.
After signing in again, she successfully accessed the CRM.

## Resolution Notes

The issue was caused by missing user permissions rather than
a CRM platform failure.

The access change was documented to maintain an accurate record
of the user's permissions.

## Ticket Closure

- **Status:** Resolved
- **Resolution:** CRM access restored
- **Root Cause:** Missing CRM access group membership
- **Follow-up Required:** No
