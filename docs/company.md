# Borealis Travel — IT Environment

## Company Overview

Borealis Travel is a fictional premium travel company that provides
high-end travel planning and concierge services to clients worldwide.

The company operates through several departments that rely heavily
on technology and internal business applications to manage daily
operations and provide service to customers.

## Departments

Borealis Travel has approximately 100 employees distributed across
the following departments:

- Reservations
- Concierge
- Sales
- Marketing
- Operations
- Administration

## IT Environment

Borealis Travel relies on a centralized IT environment to support
employees across all departments.

The IT environment includes:

- Windows laptops and desktop computers
- Microsoft 365 for email, productivity and collaboration
- CRM platform for managing customer and travel information
- Front for internal and customer communication
- Zoom for meetings and remote communication
- Shared network/cloud drives for company documents
- Company Portal for software and device management

The IT department is responsible for maintaining these systems,
supporting users, managing access and permissions, troubleshooting
technical issues, documenting incidents and escalating complex
problems when necessary.

## IT Support Responsibilities

The IT department provides first-line technical support to all
employees.

Typical responsibilities include:

- Troubleshooting hardware and software issues
- Managing user accounts and system access
- Installing and configuring software
- Managing Microsoft 365 accounts and permissions
- Supporting business applications such as the CRM and Front
- Troubleshooting Zoom and communication issues
- Managing employee onboarding and offboarding
- Monitoring and resolving helpdesk tickets
- Documenting incidents and their resolutions
- Escalating complex issues to vendors or specialized teams
- Identifying recurring problems and recommending improvements
